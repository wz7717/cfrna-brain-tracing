## Public example design

Both inputs contain the 21,668 public gene identifiers packaged in the frozen
regional reference. Synthetic values are deterministic SHA-256 functions of
the gene identifier and a v0.1.16-specific prefix. They do not derive from a
donor, patient, tissue, disease cohort or the restricted Bo2023 processed
author-package matrices, and they were not optimized for an anatomical answer.

- Network overlap: 200/200 for both examples
- Regional-reference overlap: 21,668 genes for both examples
- Counts input source: `raw_counts`
- logCPM input source: `logcpm`
- Expected outputs: generated by the locked production route and verified
  against committed JSON; fields, ordering, categorical identities, lock and
  route are exact, while floating-point values use `math.isclose()` with
  `rel_tol=1e-10` and `abs_tol=1e-12` for cross-platform last-bit drift
